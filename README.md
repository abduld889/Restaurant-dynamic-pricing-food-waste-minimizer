# Restaurant-dynamic-pricing-food-waste-minimizer
AI-powered restaurant menu pricing and food-waste optimization workflow built with n8n, PostgreSQL, Google Gemini, Firecrawl, and Slack.

## Overview

The Restaurant Chain Dynamic Menu Pricing & Food-Waste Minimizer is an AI-powered restaurant operations automation project developed by Group 8 as part of the TechCrush AI Automation Capstone.

The project addresses two major challenges faced by restaurant chains: food waste caused by perishable inventory and inefficient menu pricing that may not reflect changing stock levels, expiry dates, and competitor prices.

Built with n8n, PostgreSQL, Google Gemini AI, Firecrawl, and Slack, the system automates inventory analysis, competitor price research, pricing recommendations, and daily management reporting.

Using a Lagos-based restaurant chain as its demonstration environment, the workflow analyzes restaurant inventory and menu data across multiple locations, identifies potential food-waste risks, and generates AI-assisted pricing recommendations, including opportunities for discounts and Chef's Specials.

The workflow then delivers structured reports to restaurant management through Slack, helping decision-makers respond to inventory conditions and pricing opportunities more efficiently.

This project demonstrates how AI automation can support smarter restaurant operations, reduce avoidable food waste, and improve decision-making across multiple restaurant branches.

**Note:** This is a capstone prototype. It generates recommendations and management reports; it does not directly modify live restaurant POS prices.

## Project Objectives

- Identify ingredients and menu items at risk of food waste.
- Analyze inventory levels and expiry dates.
- Research competitor menu pricing.
- Generate AI-assisted menu pricing recommendations.
- Identify potential discounts and Chef's Specials.
- Deliver daily reports to restaurant management through Slack.
- Demonstrate scalable automation across multiple restaurant locations.

## Technology Stack

- n8n — Workflow automation
- PostgreSQL — Inventory and menu data management
- Google Gemini AI — Pricing decisions and recommendations
- Firecrawl — Competitor menu research
- Slack — Automated management notifications
- JavaScript — Data processing and workflow calculations
